# Scientific Evidence and Support

## Quantum Mechanics Evidence

### Bell's Inequality Violations

**Experiments**: Aspect (1982), Delft (2015), Vienna/NIST loophole-free tests

**Findings**:
- Entangled particles show correlations exceeding classical bounds
- Cannot be explained by local hidden variables
- Suggests information transcends spatial separation

**ISC Interpretation**: Information exists more fundamentally than particles; quantum correlations reflect underlying informational connections.

### Quantum Eraser Experiments

**Experiments**: Scully & Druhl, Kim et al.

**Findings**:
- Which-path information determines wave/particle behavior
- Works even when information erased after detection
- Retroactive determination based on available information

**ISC Interpretation**: Physical behavior determined by informational state, not material properties.

### Kochen-Specker Theorem

**Proof**: Mathematical demonstration that quantum observables cannot all have pre-existing definite values.

**Implication**: Reality consists of potentialities becoming actual through informational interactions, not objects with intrinsic properties.

## Information Theory in Physics

### Holographic Principle

**Origin**: 't Hooft, Susskind; black hole thermodynamics

**Finding**: All information in a volume can be encoded on its boundary surface.

**ISC Interpretation**: Three-dimensional space may be emergent from two-dimensional information; dimensionality is not fundamental.

### AdS/CFT Correspondence

**Discovery**: Maldacena (1997)

**Finding**: Gravitational theory in curved spacetime equivalent to quantum field theory on boundary.

**ISC Interpretation**: Demonstrates how seemingly different physical descriptions represent same underlying informational reality.

### Black Hole Information Paradox

**Problem**: Hawking showed black holes evaporate, apparently destroying information (violating quantum mechanics).

**Resolution Attempts**: Information encoded in Hawking radiation, ER=EPR proposal

**ISC Interpretation**: Information conservation may be more fundamental than energy conservation; information is irreducible.

### Landauer's Principle

**Finding**: Erasing information requires minimum energy dissipation.

**Implication**: Direct physical link between information and thermodynamics; information processing has physical consequences.

## Digital Physics Support

### Cellular Automata Complexity

**Work**: Wolfram's Rule 110 (Turing complete)

**Finding**: Extremely simple rules generate arbitrary computational complexity.

**ISC Interpretation**: Complex physical phenomena may emerge from simple informational rules.

### Computational Equivalence

**Principle** (Wolfram): Almost all non-trivial processes are computationally equivalent.

**Implication**: Human intelligence is one computational process among many, not privileged category.

### Lattice Gauge Theory Success

**Finding**: Discrete computational models accurately predict continuous physical phenomena in QCD.

**ISC Interpretation**: Discreteness at fundamental level may give rise to apparent continuity.

## Consciousness Research Support

### Anesthesia Studies

**Finding**: Loss of consciousness correlates with breakdown of specific informational relationships (feedback connections), not reduced neural activity.

**ISC Interpretation**: Consciousness tracks informational integration patterns, not substrate activity levels.

### Psychedelic Research

**Finding**: Altered states correspond to predictable changes in brain information flow and integration.

**ISC Interpretation**: Conscious experience directly tied to informational pattern properties.

### Global Workspace Theory

**Finding**: Consciousness correlates with widespread activation and integration across brain regions.

**ISC Interpretation**: Consciousness emerges from information integration, supporting phi-based theories.

### IIT Experimental Tests

**Ongoing**: Tononi's group testing phi predictions against consciousness measures.

**Preliminary**: Phi correlates with consciousness across states (wake, sleep, anesthesia).

## Quantum Biology Evidence

### Photosynthesis

**Finding**: Quantum coherence in photosynthetic light harvesting at room temperature.

**Implication**: Biological systems may exploit quantum information processing.

### Avian Navigation

**Finding**: Bird magnetoreception may involve quantum entanglement in cryptochrome proteins.

**Implication**: Quantum effects in biological neural systems possible.

### Neural Microtubules

**Hypothesis** (Penrose-Hameroff): Quantum processing in neural microtubules.

**Status**: Controversial but not ruled out; active research area.

## Mathematical Structure Evidence

### Unreasonable Effectiveness of Mathematics

**Observation** (Wigner): Mathematics describes physics with unreasonable precision.

**ISC Interpretation**: If reality is mathematical/informational, our models directly describe underlying structure.

### Amplituhedron

**Discovery**: Arkani-Hamed, Trnka

**Finding**: Complex particle interactions derivable from elegant mathematical structure without referencing spacetime.

**ISC Interpretation**: Physical reality may be mathematical structure, not objects in spacetime.

## Limitations and Challenges

### What ISC Cannot Yet Explain

1. **Specific phi threshold** for consciousness emergence
2. **Why particular patterns** have particular qualia
3. **How to test** the core metaphysical claim directly
4. **The combination problem**: micro to macro consciousness

### Competing Interpretations

Each piece of evidence has alternative explanations:
- Many-worlds interpretation of quantum mechanics
- Materialist consciousness theories (Global Workspace, Higher-Order)
- Mathematical formalism without ontological commitment

ISC presents one coherent framework unifying these observations, but alternatives exist.

## Testable Predictions

1. **Phi-consciousness correlation**: Refined phi measures should predict consciousness levels across conditions
2. **Substrate independence**: Same informational patterns in different substrates should produce equivalent cognitive capabilities
3. **Physical constant derivability**: Currently "arbitrary" constants should prove derivable from information-theoretic principles
4. **Quantum neural correlates**: Consciousness-related brain activity should show non-local quantum signatures
